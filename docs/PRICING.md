# Pricing & go-to-market

## Ideal customer
Companies with 200–5,000 employees, 100+ SaaS tools and growing AI use, but **no full-time enterprise architecture team**:
scale-ups, PE-backed mid-market, and regulated firms (energy, telco, financial services, health) facing AI Act / privacy audits.

## Wedge: the 2-week savings assessment
Fixed fee **$9,500**, credited against year one. Read-only connectors to SSO, invoices/expenses and cloud billing; deliverables are the
shadow-IT list, renewal traps, overlap clusters with business cases, and an executive briefing. Target: show at least 10x the fee
in identified savings, then convert to a subscription.

The in-app **Return on subscription** tile compares identified and approved savings with the plan fee (`PLAN_NAME`,
`PLAN_FEE_USD`). In the NorthGrid demo: about $2.8M identified against $60k a year, roughly 47x.

## Why customers buy
1. **It pays for itself.** Savings are measured and audited (identified → approved → realised).
2. **Audit-ready AI governance** without hiring a team: risk tiers cite rules, and evidence packs name the approvers.
3. **Trust.** Read-only connectors, a human approves every change, every number cites its source, and there is a full audit log.

## Expansion path
Assessment → Starter (savings) → Growth (add AI governance when the first audit or AI Act question arrives) → Enterprise.
